# Just Putty Explosives

**Just Putty Explosives** adds deployable explosive putty to Minecraft. Stick it to walls, floors, and ceilings — then wire it up with blasting caps and a remote detonator for some serious boom.

*Requires Forge 1.20.1*

## What's in the Mod

### Explosive Putty
Comes in three explosive flavors:

- **Explosive Putty** — reliable all-rounder
- **Incendiary Explosive Putty** — same blast, but sets fires (won't burn underwater)
- **High Explosive Putty** — twice the boom, twice the crater

Putty sticks to almost any surface and blows up whatever catches it in the blast — including other putty, so you can build chain reactions.

### Blasting Caps
A Blasting Cap arms any putty, turning it into its rigged variant. The putty keeps its type — incendiary stays incendiary, high stays high.

### The Detonator
The fun part. A pocket remote that links to up to **5 rigged explosives** at once:

- Right-click a rigged putty to link it. Right-click again to unlink.
- Right-click the air (or anything that isn't rigged putty) to detonate your whole setup.
- Each putty costs 1 durability to detonate — a full five-pack burns through a third of the detonator's charge.
- Links belong to the detonator itself, so you can hand it to a friend or pick it back up after relogging.

Once you hit detonate, there's a short fuse delay while the charge sound plays — and once it's primed, nobody can steal your links. You *can* still break a primed putty to cancel its blast, which makes for some great last-second saves.

### Secret Pot Bombs 🏺
Place putty on top of a decorated pot and it disappears completely inside. Rig it, link it, and you've got a bomb nobody sees coming.

## Installation

1. Install [Forge](https://files.minecraftforge.net/) for Minecraft 1.20.1
2. Drop `just_putty_explosive-1.0.0-forge-1.20.1.jar` into your `mods` folder
3. Launch and grab everything from the mod's creative tab

## Known Quirks

- Rigged putty variants don't appear in the creative tab — they're meant to be crafted via blasting caps
- Putty only attaches where it physically touches a block — fence tops are fair game, fence sides are not
- In creative mode, blasting caps are free (because of course they are)
